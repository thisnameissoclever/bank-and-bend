Reviewed 77 waterbodies represented by 99 access records. This covers all 98 lake, pond and reservoir entries after correcting six reservoir shores previously classified as rivers, plus Swift Power Canal. The grouped findings, events and source links are preserved in [the evidence record](2026-11-01-stocking-evidence.json). Each corresponding access now has a separate structured stocking review.

These are planned windows. Annual allocations shared between earlier and later months have a null future quantity where the remaining share is unspecified.

- ODFW schedules 1,000 legal-size trout for Walter Wirth during November 2-6 and 1,000 for Turner during November 9-13. Hagg's latest planned week is October 19-23, with 3,000 legal-size and 1,100 trophy trout. Completed delivery was not established for those plans.
- Agency reports confirm earlier plants at Vernonia, Turner, Blue Lake and St. Louis Ponds, with the published date precision and quantities preserved. Blue Lake and St. Louis received channel catfish in July. Catfish stocking does not raise this application's accepted-species prospects.
- Hartman's 2027 plan uses the alias HARTMAN PD (WAHKEENA LK). West Salish stocking remains separate from East Salish. Main Klineline records are not assigned to East Pond. Unrelated McNary Channel Ponds, Smith River Reservoir and same-named lakes in other counties were excluded.

## Sources and retrieval

Washington evidence uses the [WDFW Fish Plants dataset](https://data.wa.gov/Fish-and-Wildlife/WDFW-Fish-Plants/6fex-3r7d), [catchable plant tables](https://wdfw.wa.gov/fishing/reports/stocking/trout-plants/all), lake descriptions and the [2026 annual stocking plan](https://wdfw.wa.gov/sites/default/files/publications/02693/wdfw02693.pdf). The full actual-data query covered Clark, Cowlitz, Lewis, Skamania, Klickitat and Thurston counties. The annual plan adds useful future monthly windows and size allocations beyond the recent-delivery list. Oregon evidence uses water-specific [ODFW stocking schedule queries](https://myodfw.com/fishing/species/trout/stocking-schedule), current regional recreation reports, archived dated agency reports, fishery guides and private managers. The statewide printable table stopped at 500 rows, so water-specific queries were used to avoid truncating later events. Announced 2027 plans are retained where present. A stocking aggregator was considered as a secondary cross-check; it did not turn planned dates into confirmations. Repeated WDFW rows were cross-checked against the catchable tables. Merwin's July 13 total of 3,464 rainbow at 0.70 fish per pound and Fort Borst's April 23 total of 6,240 at 2.60 fish per pound were corroborated. Two identical May 5 Klineline rows remain ambiguous in both views. The review displays that 2,000-fish batch once with a note and does not publish a summed annual total from it. Web-reader failures for agency tables and the annual PDF were recovered through the agencies' public data and direct downloads. Rainbow Trout Farm's website returned HTTP 403. The current agency license record and independent fishery descriptions establish its private-managed character, while replenishment dates and quantities remain unavailable. The latest lake-specific high-lake documents found for Mirror and Lower Twin remain dated 2023; those dates are explicit.

## Scope and verification

This review changes stocking information and six water-type labels. Existing source claims and checked dates remain intact. No availability override, access certification, advisory clearance, monthly score, temporary score expiry or private user record was rewritten. A dated stocking report provides no independent reopening evidence. The stocking detail panel separates confirmed and scheduled events, supports month/week intervals and unknown quantities, and keeps older events collapsible. Data tests require a dated review and resolvable event sources for every stillwater. Schedule dates never become confirmed merely by passing.
