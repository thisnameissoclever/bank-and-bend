## Meaning of the score

an angler clarified that being unlikely to be seen is the primary purpose of the personal-space factor. The interface now calls it Privacy & space. Each location's score considers observer sightlines, vegetation and terrain screening, nearby paths and recreation, and likely visitor use. Room to spread out remains secondary. Audible noise stays in Quiet setting. The score is a practical judgment about the recorded fishing position or explicitly described public bank area. A shared open platform can score poorly even where the wider park is forested. A long exposed lawn can offer excellent spacing and little concealment. Strongly screened walk-in positions can score highly even though an occasional walker or boat could pass. The same saved seclusion key and owner importance continue to control this factor. Existing score filters and sorts now use the privacy assessment. The earlier physical-separation judgment remains separate for the existing severe-space caps. A low privacy rating affects the weighted average without creating a new hard cap.

## Evidence and limits

The dated [decision record](2026-10-31-privacy-decisions.json) records each assessment, source IDs, limitations and retained physical-room score. The corresponding sources and current reasoning live in data/spots.json and the site's Sources and Overview panels. The review reconsidered bank descriptions, access audits, documented trails and recreation facilities, mapped vegetation, aerial imagery and firsthand observations. Fresh aerial exports were examined for the Oregon groups and selected Washington banks. Existing access-audit imagery and source descriptions supplied additional layout evidence. Newly retrieved imagery URLs are attached to the locations where used. Image acquisition dates were unavailable. Aerial canopy supports screening inferences, but it can hide gaps at standing height. Deciduous leaf loss, changing river levels, elevated paths and opposite-bank homes can alter exposure. Facility capacities identify possible observer concentrations; they are not visitor counts. an angler's quiet or empty visits support their recorded conditions and remain distinct from a guarantee of concealment. Where an old fishing pin was withdrawn, a general marker was excluded from precise bank-level visual claims. A documented wooded or exposed public corridor can still support a useful area-level judgment. A null score is retained where the actual usable bank remains unidentified or construction and restoration have changed the position that would need to be assessed. The ratings describe the bank layout under ordinary use when legally available. Existing closures and unresolved access remain separate. No privacy score establishes reopening, retention legality, current water quality or a usable replacement navigation pin.
