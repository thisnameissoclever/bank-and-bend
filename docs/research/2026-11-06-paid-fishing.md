# Paid fishing coverage, November 6, 2026

Added a paid-fishing classification, map badge, fee panel and three-way filter. Private pay-for-catch ponds, fishing day access, memberships and guest fishing count as paid. Public parking passes and state licenses alone do not.

The review adds 33 locations and tags five existing locations, giving 38 paid records. Existing location IDs, private journals, favorites, scoring weights and availability revisions are preserved. The new filter defaults to All for older saved preferences and shared links. Unknown walking distances remain unknown.

## Coverage and evidence

- Reviewed Rainbow Trout Farm, Horning’s Hideout, Indian Springs, Zeek Creek, private Silver Lake access, Offut Lake Resort, private Black Lake access and the Oregon Fishing Club public property catalog.
- Included 26 club properties using static road estimates from generic Vancouver city center, with an outer allowance of 135 minutes for the requested approximate two-hour range. These are research-scope estimates, not live traffic predictions or stored personal travel times.
- Kept private club entrances unresolved where public map markers and individually inspected imagery do not establish the member parking-to-bank route. Each record describes the visible obstacle. No member directions, gate codes or private access instructions were obtained or published.
- Checked 34 aerial frames, plus Offut and Silver Cove operator site maps. Existing supported pins remain intact. New supported resort pins are approximate and require check-in and staff directions.
- Preserved Horning’s regular-fishing seasonal closure. The separate winter session is fly-only and catch-and-release.
- Indian Springs remains inactive: current booking confirmation is unavailable and sources conflict about opening. Zeek Creek is a licensed U-Catch lead with no verified current customer entrance or hours.
- Black Lake Grocery and Salmon Shores fees are visitor reports, explicitly unconfirmed with the operators. Columbus Park admission is published, but the current permitted fishing section needs confirmation separately from the swimming area.
- Retrieved WDFW 2026 delivery records for Offutt Lake and Black Lake, filtered by exact Thurston County waterbody name. Shared existing Silver Lake stocking and advisory evidence with the additional resort access, retaining original dates. Private stocking descriptions were not converted into dated deliveries.

Individual fees, source URLs, retrieval dates, visual findings and access gaps are attached to each record in `data/spots.json`. The route and exclusion ledger is `data/paid-fishing-review-2026-11-06.json`. The search does not establish that every unadvertised private fishing arrangement has been found.
