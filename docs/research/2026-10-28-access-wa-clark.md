# Clark County and Lewis River reservoir access audit

Checked 2026-10-28. Every one of the 59 original entries was inspected on newly retrieved aerial imagery. Returned export extents, rather than requested bounds, were used for coordinate conversion. Manager pages, public-land boundaries, trail maps and current closure notices were checked. Tree cover was accepted where a manager map or photographed route supplied the missing layout. Supported means the named public fishing access and approach are established. A null exact pin remains null even when the broader site is supported. Unresolved does not mean private. Blocked means a documented current closure. Seasonal and fishing closures were preserved.
