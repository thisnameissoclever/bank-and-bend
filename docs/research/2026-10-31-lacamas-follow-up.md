an angler requested a concrete answer about access. Current documents establish public parking and ordinary shoreline access at Heritage Park and Leadbetter. The 2026 construction closures are at Round Lake's dam and northeast bridge. The six-foot drawdown does not independently establish that all Lacamas Lake banks are inaccessible. The actual open question is narrow: at each access, does firm public ground still let a bank angler cast into fishable water at the present lowered level?
