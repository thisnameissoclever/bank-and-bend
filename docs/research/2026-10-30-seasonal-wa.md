## Other findings

- [WDFW's October 29 weir table](https://wdfw.wa.gov/fishing/reports/creel/southwest) now marks the Washougal Fords and Coweeman weirs removed. The October 26 PDF still said installed. Elochoman, South Fork Toutle and Green/North Fork Toutle structures remain installed. Removal alone establishes neither a new bite nor public bank access.
- [USGS East Fork Lewis gauge](https://waterdata.usgs.gov/monitoring-location/USGS-14222500) showed provisional discharge rising from 38.7 cfs on October 23 at 00:00 PDT to 217 cfs on October 30 at 08:00 PDT. The source record cites the actual interval query. Flow context does not establish keeper catches or water speed at individual banks. East Fork monthly judgments remain 5 or 6; North Fork creel catches were not applied to it.
- The [October 19-25 creel report](https://wdfw.wa.gov/sites/default/files/2026-10/swwa-fishing-reports-oct-19-25-2026.pdf) had four Washougal bank anglers, no retained fish and three Chinook/jacks released. This small sample did not justify resetting every ordinary monthly reach score. Several Columbia sections had no bank effort reported, which is distinct from zero catches per surveyed angler. Mainstem Kalama had one keeper per six bank anglers. Bonneville had nine per 12, without implying that upstream Gorge beaches share that rate.
- Confirmed 2026 actual releases include Swofford 27,600 rainbow, Deep Lake in Thurston County 8,505 rainbow, Swift Reservoir 37,900 rainbow, Swift Power Canal 10,500 rainbow, Horsethief 18,000 standard rainbow plus 100 broodstock, and Plummer 3,000 rainbow. These are dated spring/early-summer deliveries, with no estimate of remaining fish. Merwin's October 1 kokanee entry is 45,000 at eight fish per pound, too small to establish immediate dinner fishing; its July large-rainbow records remain relevant.
- Freshly read [steelhead release records](https://wdfw.wa.gov/fishing/reports/stocking/steelhead) support upcoming winter cohort context. Salmon Creek's March 2026 bulletin explains later-returning winter stock and the final spring 2025 smolt release. Its exceptional spring opening was not extended into 2027.
- Newly considered public Fishbrain entries support trout at the correct North Bonneville Gillette Lake and useful-size smallmouth at Greenleaf Slough. Dates and methods are missing, so no fresh-catch claim was made. The Stevens County Lake Gillette and an uncorroborated tourism golden-trout claim were excluded.
- November closures at Horsethief, Swift Power Canal and the upper White Salmon retention reach remain unchanged. No water-quality finding or access pause was overridden by this seasonal research.
