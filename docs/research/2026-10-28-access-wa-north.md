# Northern Washington access audit

Checked October 28, 2026. All 53 assigned records received an individual manager-source review and fresh aerial inspection. Coordinates were placed using the image service's returned geographic bounds. Public access rights were assessed independently from visible physical layout. Results: 47 supported public or owner-permitted fisheries, 5 unresolved access routes, and 1 currently blocked shore route. Existing fishery closures and advisory restrictions remain in place. Supported public fisheries can retain a null exact pin when a manager establishes the access but trees or unlabeled parking obscure the final position. Null pins are withheld from navigation. A supported access finding does not establish current fish retention, water quality, or a current waterline. The primary corrections include the actual County Line Park, Brick Wagner Park at Tilley Avenue, Gerhart Gardens north launch, the public-only Sportsman's Club footprint, and landward replacements for several river pins. Prichard, Red Barn, Olequa, Toledo First Street and Satterlund remain inactive until the material access question is resolved. Mossyrock Park's selected beach approach is inactive under its current closure.

## Method and evidence limits

- Every original and replacement image was visually reviewed. Old map-source interpretations remain in the corpus with an explicit superseded label.
- WDFW polygons were used for its public-access parcels; Cowlitz County assessor parcels were checked for disputed ownership and boundaries. A facility point alone was insufficient.
- Ground photographs and park maps were consulted where available. County Line and Hog Island received manager ground-photo checks.
- Imagery acquisition dates are not supplied. Seasonal flow, vegetation and gate changes remain possible. No claim of an on-site inspection is made.
- Scores changed only where a corrected location, restricted public footprint or unresolved position invalidated the previous basis. Catch prospects and seasonal evidence were preserved.
