## Method

The estimates use the fishery's stocking program, plausible useful fish sizes, seasonal habitat, conventional methods and water reachable from the specific bank. Sparse recent catch reporting changes confidence. A seasonal reduction requires a biological, legal or physical reason. Evidence confidence is separate from the fishing score. Personal space measures the ability to find roughly 50-100 feet between anglers. Quietness measures road, boat and park noise. North Fork's launch platform and Hartman's compact dock retain their poor space ratings and corresponding overall-fit caps. The strongest general fisheries in this group are Hagg, North Fork, Trillium and Trojan. Trojan's spring trophy program raises its general potential; shallow-water summer conditions make its October prospects substantially weaker. Faraday's stocked shore fishery earns 7 for both periods. The evidence supports reachable hatchery trout, with less evidence of substantial fish size than the trophy-oriented lakes.

## Evidence

- [Hagg agency schedule](https://myodfw.com/fishing/species/trout/stocking-schedule-print), checked October 24: 3,000 legal and 1,100 trophy trout planned for October 19-23. The October 21 Marine Board report in the existing record gives 28% capacity, which affects the bank approach. No present fish inventory is inferred from the planned count.
- [Faraday owner page](https://portlandgeneral.com/about/parks-campgrounds/faraday-lake), checked October 24: year-round trout stocking, abundant shore access and a boat prohibition. The October 28-December 4 road-work notice preserves lake access and limits travel beyond the east-end bridge. The owner map and a dated walking route place the selected parking and fishing platform before that farther crossing. The September 28 schedule mirror lists 1,400 trout; delivery remains unconfirmed.
- [North Fork bank guide](https://www.bestfishinginamerica.com/OR-north-fork-reservoir-rainbow-trout.html), 2026 edition checked October 24: typical 10-12-inch trout, personal catches around 14 inches, lower-launch bank fishing and improved fall opportunity until the October 31 closure. The personal catch method is unspecified. The existing agency schedule records three September plans totaling 10,902 legal trout.
- [Trillium guide](https://www.bestfishinginamerica.com/or-trillium-lake-fishing-mount-hood-oregon.html), newly considered October 24: a roughly 60-acre lake, deeper water near the dam, recurring larger trout and simple bait/lure methods. Its schedule is explicitly 2025. The existing June 10, 2026 agency-attributed report confirms more than 6,000 stocked rainbows; the August 24 listing is secondary evidence.
- [Vernonia guide](https://www.bestfishinginamerica.com/or-vernonia-pond-fishing-oregon.html), 2026 edition newly considered October 24: shallow warm-season trout limitation, shore crappie advice attributed to Oregon Bass & Panfish Club angler Bob Judkins, and frequently small perch. The agency guide describes useful spring and early-summer trout fishing. The author's June pessimism is treated as judgment, with the shared shallow-water seasonal pattern carrying the rating.
- [Trojan guide](https://www.bestfishinginamerica.com/or-recreation-lake-trojan-pond-fishing-oregon.html), 2026 edition in the existing record: spring legal and trophy plants, shoreline access and warm-season transition to panfish. The preserved visitor account records eight 8-10-inch trout in four hours during a spring outing.
- [Benson and Hartman guide](https://www.bestfishinginamerica.com/or-benson-lake-hartman-pond-fishing-oregon.html), 2025 edition rechecked October 24: seasonal stocked trout, resident warmwater fish, Benson west-bank openings and Hartman east docks. Highway and railway noise justify low quietness scores independently of fishing potential. Its stocking counts retain their 2025 date. Three additional source checks inform the interpretation:

- [Oregon Department of Fish and Wildlife trophy-trout explanation](https://www.dfw.state.or.us/news/2017/06_jun/062817c.asp), June 28, 2017: historical category definitions put trophy trout at 15 inches and allow legal trout release at 8 inches. This supports caution about treating every legal trout as a substantial dinner fish.
- [2026 trophy-program review](https://www.bestfishinginamerica.com/news-oregon-300-tons-of-trophy-trout-stocked-in-oregon-fishing-lakes-22-top-spots-listed.html), checked October 24: the author's schedule review lists annual plans of 12,950 Hagg and 5,500 Trojan trophy trout. These corroborate the importance of larger fish to the programs. An unrelated geographic error reduces confidence in uncorroborated details.
- [Northwest Fly Fishing Outfitters fall assessment](https://www.flyshopnw.com/blogs/nwffo-blog/oregon-washington-september-trout-stocking-schedules), September 11, 2026: a regional retailer expects repeatedly stocked stillwaters to keep producing into fall. The guide's exact assigned dates and Faraday quantity were excluded because they differ from later schedule evidence. The [current agency report](https://myodfw.com/recreation-report/fishing-report/willamette-zone) supplied no new lake-specific result for these eight records.

## Limits

The assessments preserve existing access points and personal-space restrictions. This audit did not repeat imagery, advisory or complete legal checks. Each published location contains dated source references. Published keeper counts, planned stockings and historical fishery patterns remain distinct. The October scores need revision when the season changes. North Fork closes to trout fishing after October 31. Trillium's snow, ice and road access belong in trip availability. Faraday road work can affect access and quietness. Hagg drawdown can alter the route from the supported shore-entry point to the water.

[Faraday walking route](https://www.mywildadventure.com/post/faraday-lake), published October 6, 2025 from a July 26 visit, independently distinguishes the parking footbridge from the farther lake bridge. The route helps interpret the owner's roadwork boundary. The precise barricade position remains unpublished.
