# Washington east and Gorge land-access audit

2026-10-28; 32 records. Outcome: 27 supported public accesses, including 23 with both supported coordinate pins retained. Four supported public accesses withhold one or both exact pins. Four records remain unresolved and one is blocked by the current campground closure. Existing separate fishing-rule and seasonal closures are preserved. Ten parking coordinates and sixteen fishing coordinates were corrected. Every original parking and fishing point was compared with an individually inspected Esri World Imagery export. Corrected coordinates use the export response extent in EPSG:4326, not the requested bounding box. Aerial capture dates and present waterlines were not established. Manager descriptions and maps establish permission; imagery establishes physical layout. Older fishing rules, seasonal profiles and water-quality checks keep their original dates. New source classes include WDFW managed access boundaries, county assessor parcel geometry, the city shoreline-plan map and county parking code. Public parcel-owner names are used only for public agencies. No private owner details are included.
