# Washington area-link audit, November 1, 2026

Reviewed all 145 Washington records in the source corpus for semantic name, region and center consistency. All 145 have a general-area name and unique center coordinates. Every one of the 108 non-null fishing pins exactly equals the corresponding area center. The remaining 37 retain an approximate area center while their exact fish pin is withheld. No new coordinate correction is supported by this audit. This is a corpus-wide label and coordinate-consistency review with focused source checks. It does not claim 145 Google search results or 145 parking-to-bank routes were reverified today. County Line's exact route is being investigated separately. Two region corrections are supported: I-5 / Mandy Road and Wallace Bar both say Cowlitz County, but WDFW explicitly identifies Lewis County. Proposed values and sources are in wa-area-proposals-2026-11-01.json. Direct WDFW reopening returned HTTP 403 after official-page search extraction supplied the county labels. No access status or review date should change based on these geography-only checks. Eight repeated-name groups cover 22 records: Silver Lake (3), Mayfield Lake (3), Stevenson waterfront (2), Daybreak Regional Park (2), Lake River (2), Vancouver Lake (2), Lewis River (3), and East Fork Lewis (5). These names can legitimately describe the general area, but they cannot identify a particular access by unconstrained text search. Coordinate-centered fallback resolves that ambiguity without inventing place IDs or replacing withheld precise pins. Targeted checks also found no alias correction necessary for Martin Bar / Lions Community Park. The Estuary Partnership explicitly lists Martin Bar and Woodland Bar as names for Lions Community Park. PacifiCorp's current recreation page names Eagle Cliff Park and Johnson Creek River Access consistently with the corpus. WDFW names Swift Power Canal in Skamania County, consistent with the corpus. Little Ash versus Little Ashes is a known agency/map naming variation, not a different lake.
