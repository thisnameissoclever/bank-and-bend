## Method

General keeper potential describes practical bank fishing during a normal favorable fishing period. Current-season prospects describe late October 2026. Both use an angler's species, dinner-size, methods and firsthand experience. Research confidence remains a separate factor. A missing recent catch report alone causes no fishing-score deduction. Personal space measures room for roughly 50-100 ft separation. Quietness measures road noise, recreational bustle and peaceful surroundings. These estimates apply to the supported access and its usable adjacent bank.

## Evidence findings

Carlisle gains the clearest new size evidence. The Chronicle's original April 24, 2026 report confirms that Onalaska students released more than 300 rainbow trout described as seven pounds each on April 23. This is a completed release into a lake with established public shore access. Current survival and distribution remain uncertain. General keeper potential rises to 8; late-October prospects are 6. Horseshoe and Sacajawea have broad usable public shorelines. Their space scores rise to 8. Highway noise at Horseshoe and ordinary city-park activity at Sacajawea belong in quietness. Sacajawea's shallow warm-water summer slowdown supplies a biological reason for its current-season 5, while recurring cool-season trout plants support general keeper potential of 7. Wallace's small area, substantial catchable-trout stocking and multiple bank options support general keeper potential of 8. A new local park article documents recurring bloom concerns in hot summer and early fall. Current advisory status remains unresolved, so the app should continue displaying that condition prominently with its fishing score. Mayfield's reservoir operator independently confirms annual rainbow stocking. A practical fishing guide describes ordinary bait fishing from the bank, good shore access at Ike Kinswa and renewed fall trout activity. The August stocking record includes fish around 0.9 lb. An October 6 retailer report describes good trout fishing, with considerable boat-oriented advice. Together these justify general potential of 8 and late-October prospects of 7. The individual dock and limited shore alternatives keep personal space at 6. Kress is an established cool-water trout fishery with workable bank positions. WDFW explicitly identifies late fall through late spring as its favorable period. General potential rises to 7, and late October is a moderate 6. an angler's several-hour blank visit remains prominent. Battle Ground's frequent trout plants and ordinary half-pound fish support general potential of 8. Its known full parking lot, shared pier and small bank openings keep space at 3. Haapa remains 5 for both general and current keeper potential. The October 12-18 Lewis River survey recorded 49 retained salmon or jacks among 123 bank anglers, approximately 0.40 fish per surveyed angler. an angler's repeated tiny-trout results, unresponsive salmon and 14-inch trout minimum are substantial local limits. His demonstrated personal-space and atmosphere fit support space 8 and quietness 9.

## Assessment scope


The strongest numerical changes follow a corrected definition of general keeper potential. Scores of 8 denote a strong managed fishery during favorable normal conditions. The separate late-October column gives an immediate seasonal comparison. A high fishery score still sits alongside personal-space, current-water and advisory constraints in the overall decision. Existing October 24 imagery inspection was reused for physical layout. This rating pass made no new satellite inspection. New sources considered:

Undated guide, checked October 24.
- [LewisTalk: South County Park in Toledo](https://www.lewistalk.com/2024/06/25/unwind-and-explore-in-lewis-county-your-guide-to-south-county-park-in-toledo/): Local park account describes a regularly stocked 17-acre rainbow pond, multiple fishing structures and recurrent blue-green algae concerns during hot summer and early fall. Historical bloom context warrants retaining the current advisory question.
- [The Chronicle: Onalaska students move hundreds of large trout to Carlisle Lake](https://www.chronline.com/stories/in-focus-onalaska-students-move-hundreds-of-large-trout-to-carlisle-lake,401041): Original local reporting and photo captions document the April 23, 2026 release of more than 300 rainbow trout described as seven pounds apiece, raised over 15 months. - [Tacoma Public Utilities: Mayfield Dam](https://www.mytpu.org/community-environment-parks/hydropower-natural-resources/cowlitz-river-project/mayfield-dam/): Reservoir operator confirms annual rainbow trout stocking in Mayfield Lake. Most techniques discussed involve boats, plus Rooster Tail spinners. Its recurring stocking statement has no delivery dates, so completed plants remain sourced to the existing stocking records.
- [Best Fishing in America: Battle Ground Lake fishing](https://www.bestfishinginamerica.com/washington-battle-ground-lake-fishing.html): General guide describes frequent November-May rainbow plants, some roughly one-pound fall fish, effective worm/bobber fishing, and a shared pier with small casting openings around the wooded loop. Its fishing photos and descriptions reinforce the limited room at individual positions. The guide reports summer crowds; trip-specific parking remains based on an angler. Source dates and supporting claims are recorded with each location. All reasoning remains a revisable judgment for future refreshes.
