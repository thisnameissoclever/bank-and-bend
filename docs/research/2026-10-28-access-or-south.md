# Oregon south access audit


Result: 61 supported public fishing accesses, 7 unresolved land-access candidates, 2 existing seasonal fishing closures. Checked 2026-10-28. All 70 original aerial frames were inspected, followed by wider images, manager-source searches and public-land overlays for displaced or ambiguous entries. Coordinates use the actual extent returned by the imagery service. ArcGIS may expand a requested extent to match image dimensions. No coordinates were calculated from an assumed requested extent. Image capture dates and present river levels are not established by this audit. An established public fishing park can remain supported when its exact tree-covered fishing position is null. A public boat launch alone does not establish permission or room to fish from its ramp or private neighboring docks. Unresolved legal routes are inactive. Existing fishing closures and water-quality records were preserved.
