# Clark County and Lewis reservoir coverage audit

Checked October 27, 2026, Pacific time. The audit adds 21 distinct land-access candidates and records 67 inventory decisions. New records are in `audit-wa-clark-spots.json`; the decision ledger is `audit-wa-clark-inventory.json`. The root agent owns the final merge and the separate East Fork restoration-area follow-up.

## Coverage method

Compared the existing map against Clark County's fishing and boat-launch inventories, city Camas lake access, the WDFW water-access GIS, WDFW lowland-lake fisheries, and PacifiCorp's complete Lewis River recreation inventory. Every PacifiCorp named river access and reservoir park is represented, including seasonal closures. Checked the Lower Columbia Estuary Partnership launch inventory and public whitewater-access records for small launches that ordinary fishing lists omit. Second-pass searches uncovered Yale Bridge stair access and the East Fork restoration cluster. The root agent accepted responsibility for Lower Daybreak, County Yard, Cemetery Bend, Bjur Road and Danger Park. ThreeMile/Cottrell Dam and CountyLine on the Washougal were assigned to the east-region agent. These handoffs are explicit in the ledger. The most useful additions for current bank fishing are Swift Power Canal, Island, Cedar Creek, Johnson Creek, the two hatchery accesses, Merwin Park, Yale Park and Swift Forest Camp. Swift Forest Camp and Eagle Cliff are retained as edge-of-range trips with honest drive estimates. The smaller northwest Vancouver, Leadbetter and Yale Bridge accesses remain visible with their physical limitations.

## Access and visual checks

Downloaded and visually inspected USGS NAIP aerials for every new record. The images and geographic extents are in `audit-wa-clark-imagery/`. Parking and shore coordinates are separate; shore pins remain approximate when canopy, changing water levels or new construction hide the final position. Additional visual checks:

- PacifiCorp's 2022 Johnson Creek property map establishes the public trail, separate parking entrance, adjacent WDFW land and private driveway boundaries.
- PacifiCorp's renovated Beaver Bay map establishes the 2026 day-use lot and ramp. The older aerial alone would have misrepresented the renovated facilities.
- Clark County's Lucia/Moulton trail map establishes the public parking and walking system. Fishing pins are outside the permanent falls closure buffers.
- An Esri topographic export confirms that the bridge at the Swift Canal fishing pier is Forest Road 90. A close aerial shows the north-side parking and platform.
- A current American Whitewater guide confirms Yale Bridge parking and stairs after the older 2024 damage notice. The stairs remain partly concealed in the aerial, and the final landing is explicitly approximate. A boat ramp is included when public fishing permission and a usable apron or neighboring bank are supported. Launch lanes must remain available for boats. Eagle Island is excluded because it requires a boat. Woodland Bar is an alias for the existing Martin Bar/Lions Park entry.

## Regulations and advisories

Checked current WAC 220-312-030, the 2026-27 guide's relevant lake and river sections, the emergency index and 2026 emergency filings. River records distinguish the Johnson Creek boundary, hatchery ladder exclusion and powerline/dam closure. Cedar Creek launch fishes the North Fork, not the Cedar Creek tributary. It is upstream of Johnson Creek and therefore has the May closure and April/June-November night and anti-snagging rules. Reviewed the current Washington toxic-algae list, county health guidance, Clark County's published harmful-algae map data, DOH fish-advisory publications and named-water searches. No new active inland-Lewis advisory was located. This is recorded as no advisory found, without claiming current testing or fish-tissue clearance. The Lacamas map record read October 27 has `posting_decision: none`, no current bloom report and an October 7 edit date. Its location detail is Heritage Park. It does not establish a fresh Leadbetter sample. That distinction is retained alongside the separate drawdown issue.

## Rating method and remaining limits

Every score has a specific bank-related explanation. Personal space measures physical room to separate; road and railway noise affect quietness only. Still or slow water describes the selected fishing margin, with the reason distinguishing the main channel from the bank. Monthly curves describe seasonal opportunity and legal access; they are judgments to revisit, not objective measurements. Exact current footing can change with releases, low reservoir levels, floods and vegetation. The most material unresolved details are NW 122nd Street moorage's current fee and allowed fishing boundary, the final Yale Bridge landing, rebuilt Beaver Bay edges and Lacamas refill completion. These are named in the individual records. Future updates should reconsider the reasoning, check manager access notices and regulations, then look for useful sources outside the previous list. The new source classes that materially improved this audit were the WDFW water-access GIS, the county's underlying advisory data, public whitewater-access guides and manager property/renovation maps.
