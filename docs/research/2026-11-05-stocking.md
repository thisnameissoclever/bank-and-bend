# Stocking review, November 5, 2026

Reviewed 77 waterbodies represented by 99 access records. The inventory includes 49 lake records, 22 pond records, 27 reservoir records and Swift Power Canal, which remains a river record with a separate stocking review. All 27 inactive access records were included. Shared waterbodies were researched once and the review was applied to every corresponding access.

- The current [WDFW Fish Plants dataset](https://data.wa.gov/Fish-and-Wildlife/WDFW-Fish-Plants/6fex-3r7d) and [catchable trout report](https://wdfw.wa.gov/fishing/reports/stocking/trout-plants) contain no newer matching delivery than the corpus already records. The latest relevant Clark County deliveries remain October 26 at Klineline and October 26-27 at Battle Ground, plus October 27 at Lacamas. Riffe's October 27 cutthroat release remains fry at 200 fish per pound.
- The [2026 Washington plan](https://wdfw.wa.gov/sites/default/files/publications/02693/wdfw02693.pdf) remains the current published annual plan. November and December allocations remain plans without exact delivery dates. No 2027 statewide plan was published in the [WDFW stocking-plan index](https://wdfw.wa.gov/fishing/reports/stocking/statewide) when retrieved November 5.
- The current [ODFW water-specific schedule](https://myodfw.com/fishing/species/trout/stocking-schedule) still lists 1,000 legal-size trout for Walter Wirth during November 2-6 and 1,000 for Turner during November 9-13. These are planned weeks. The current [Willamette recreation report](https://myodfw.com/recreation-report/fishing-report/willamette-zone) confirms October 26 plants at Alton Baker Canal and Row River Nature Park but does not confirm a delivery at either Walter Wirth or Turner.
- [Horning's Hideout](https://www.horningshideout.com/fishing) still says its keeper fishery is closed for the season and will reopen in May 2027. It publishes no delivery date or quantity.
- The [Oregon Aquaculture Association producer directory](https://www.oregonaquaculture.org/producers.html) was added to the Rainbow Trout Farm review as an additional source class. It corroborates the Sandy rainbow-trout producer and U-Catch operation but provides no replenishment date, quantity or current fish count.

## Judgment and preservation

No new confirmed keeper-size delivery or corrected plan changes a current-season prospect. No score, monthly profile or temporary override was changed or extended. Scheduled weeks remain scheduled even when their dates pass. No access, advisory or general checked date was refreshed. Stocking evidence did not reopen any inactive record.

The public availability endpoint returned shared revision 144 on November 5. It did not grant edit access in this session. No stocking evidence justified an availability change, so no revision-checked write or saved proposal was needed. Private visits, comments, favorites, reviews, filters, weights, sharing data and availability revisions were not modified.

Each structured stocking review now has a November 5 check date, a November 12 next-review date and a per-access research-log entry. Existing delivery history, plans, source limitations and waterbody-specific distinctions were preserved.
