# East local fishing coverage, October 27, 2026

This inventory records researched public banks, known restrictions and remaining candidates along the eastern corridor. The Port breakwater is excluded under the standing Columbia-pier preference. Steamboat Landing is included because an angler specifically requested it.

## Findings


- Steamboat Landing Park in Washougal expressly permits public fishing, has off-street parking and lists 8 a.m. to dusk hours. Its approximately 400-foot floating dock creates real access. It remains a Columbia-pier preference mismatch, which is stated rather than hidden.
- Wintler and Marine Park have actual public beaches and explicit city fishing permission. Marine Park's selected bank is east of the active ramp.
- Round Lake is distinct from the existing Lacamas Heritage entry. County live algae data reports no advisory while monitoring a bloom. The 2026 drawdown, refill and trail construction remain material current limitations.
- Fallen Leaf's reviewed public park rules explicitly prohibit fishing. It is excluded pending a genuinely separate legal public access, not labeled a poor fishing spot.
- Beacon Rock's broad park-page fishing prohibition conflicts with current WDFW Columbia rules. The full candidate is inactive until that specific conflict is resolved.
- Daybreak has real flood, bank-instability and downstream river-restoration closures. Its main park is not described as wholly closed; no safe open fishing entry was established.
- Icehouse personal space was revised from 3 to 5. Inspected imagery supports a narrow east/south access corridor roughly 100-130 meters long, with interrupted openings. Several separated anglers are physically plausible; heavy pressure and vegetation still limit flexibility. Traffic sound stays only in quietness, which remains 4. The exact Bonneville Dam-to-Hood River Bridge rule was rechecked for both Stevenson records. The October 16-31 hatchery-coho restriction applies there, and ODFW current updates independently confirm the wild-coho release requirement below Hood River Bridge.

## Evidence and pin method

Every added position has separate parking and shore-access coordinates, source references, 12-month seasonality and reasons for each rating. Pins are marked approximate because aerial imagery establishes the public approach and general shore, while some final footsteps and current water levels remain unresolved. The NAIP files and exact public service URLs are saved under `east-imagery/`; capture dates were not established. Aerial inspection does not establish current gates or legality. Attempts to retrieve Port ground photographs returned 403, so no ground-image inspection is claimed. Sources were public-manager pages, current WDFW rule text, current ODFW Columbia updates, public Clark County advisory REST records, official plans and dated historical bank reports. Historical catches establish fishery plausibility without being presented as current success. The complete WDFW emergency index returned 403; targeted official searches were used, and that limit is stated in the records. Personal space uses physical 50-100-foot separation, not audible noise. Manageable water judges whether ordinary bait, float or spinner presentations can stay in useful near-bank water. Scores describe the selected position, not the general reputation of the whole river.
