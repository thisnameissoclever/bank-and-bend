# Access audit: October 28, 2026

Reviewed all 322 maintained locations individually against manager information, public access or parcel maps, and aerial imagery. The review supports public access at 275 locations. Another 34 have unresolved material access questions; 13 have documented access or seasonal obstacles. Existing separate fishing-rule and seasonal restrictions can also keep a supported access inactive. Corrected 229 existing parking or fishing coordinates, withheld 153 unsupported coordinates, and added 3 supported points where no point existed. A coordinate correction can be a short refinement within the same public lot or a replacement for a materially wrong location. The dated decision file preserves the old coordinates solely to explain defects; they must never be reused for navigation.

## Findings

- Steel Bridge has a genuine narrow WDFW public parcel. The old fishing pin was in the river outside it. Both exact pins are withheld; the description retains the documented public approach.
- Haydu's fishing position remains on the park-side gravel bar. Parking moved into the marked lot, with the walking route updated to roughly 450-500 meters.
- Rock Cove parking moved out of the wastewater-treatment facility and into public fairgrounds parking. The bank point follows the public cove path.
- Good's Bridge remains inactive because agency inventory and current parcel information do not establish a lawful parking-to-bank route.
- Hagg Lake Sain Creek, Rood Bridge, Canby Community Park and Wilsonville Pond had misplaced coordinates corrected or withheld.
- Cook Park retains pedestrian access during its lower-lot closure. The upper parking route is supported; the exact fishing point is withheld during dock-layout work.
- Current closures include Oxbow construction, Gales Creek forest operations, Speelyai public-use closure and several seasonal shore closures. Each record retains its specific dates and sources.

## Application behavior

Parking and Fish here directions require a supported route, a supported individual pin and active availability. Unsupported points never fall back to a river center. Each location shows its access review and source links. Known public accesses can remain listed with a missing exact coordinate; the corresponding button stays disabled. Failed access reviews force inactive status even if an old saved availability override says active. The text search now has a visible notice and a clear-text control on the map. No personal filters or weights were reset. Keeper potential describes the usual dinner-fish opportunity in a favorable season. Current-season prospects describe the present month, with temporary dated evidence overriding the monthly estimate until expiry. The current scores were already above 5 at several nearby locations, including Klineline and Battle Ground at 8 and Horseshoe and Kress at 7. This audit did not inflate those scores.

## Evidence and limits

Sources newly considered include county parcel geometry, WDFW access boundaries, county parking codes, city park plans, operator fishing rules and current closure notices. Imagery was inspected for every original pin and for corrected retained positions. ArcGIS overlays use the returned extent and projection. Manager maps can support routes hidden by trees; visible paths alone do not establish permission. This access audit does not refresh every stocking, fishing-rule or water-quality check. Those fields retain their actual review dates. Water levels, gates and works can change the usable route; remaining material uncertainty is described individually.
