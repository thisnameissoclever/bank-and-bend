## Meaningful changes

**Walling Pond:** September 20, 2026 reporting identifies a stocking interruption after parking barriers prevented truck access. An agency-attributed February 12, 2025 notice corroborates that stocking would end. The 2026 water-specific schedule returned no rows. The old annual trout-stocking rationale is no longer supportable. All 12 seasonal scores become null, with the former values preserved in the dated research log. This avoids reverting to the unsupported profile when the November 13 override expires. Current informal bass fishing does not establish an accepted smallmouth fishery. The unsupported general keeper, species-fit and access-ease scores are null. The description, navigation pins and access finding were corrected in the separate access review.

**Autumn crappie:** The ODFW crappie guide directly challenges the old assumption that autumn cooling inevitably harms prospects. The 33 relevant profiles now allow a useful autumn period, with October increases of 0.5 or 1 point up to 6 and November increases of 0.5 or 1 point up to 4.5. These are modest judgments, conditional on reachable cover and dinner-size fish. No new local catch samples were invented. Other months remain unchanged.

**Sandy and Clackamas:** Nine entries receive consistent same-river overrides already used elsewhere in the corpus. The October 24 publication date and November 6 expiry remain fixed. Retrieval on October 30 does not make the report newer.

## Remaining limitations and source failures

The old Eagle Creek `what-we-do` page returned 404; the current federal production page resolved that gap. An initial Trojan schedule query used an unsuccessful waterbody label; the correct `TROJAN RECREATION LK` query returned the 2026 plan. These failures did not stop the review. Hartman's filtered query did not yield a current schedule under that name. The agency fishery guide supports spring trout and resident warmwater fish, so no fresh delivery was claimed. Lower Twin's available agency page still reports the skipped 2023 plant; a newer release was not established, and Idaho records for a same-named lake were excluded. Mirror Lake's current keeper size and ice conditions remain unresolved. The trout farm's directories disagree on hours, so no daily opening was asserted. Current Molalla and Hood bank catch samples were not found. Shared river and species evidence remains useful, without turning missing samples into poor-fishing scores. The separate access review records the adopted lower Eagle Creek order's October 31 boundary and the conflicting news-release date. This supplement does not reopen Bonnie Lure or infer any reopening from an expired notice.
