# Oregon south coverage audit

Reviewed October 27, 2026, Pacific time. Files contain 59 new fishing-access records and a named decision inventory. This is a geographic coverage pass, with lower-fit accesses retained when public bank access and an accepted keepable fishery have a reasonable basis.

- Lower Clackamas: Barton, Carver, Cross, Feldheimer and the upper McIver river access.
- Eagle Creek and Estacada: Bonnie Lure, Eagle Fern, Timber Park, the separate McIver reservoir fishing dock, Promontory and the North Fork culvert hand-launch opening.
- Molalla and Canby: Feyrer, Knights Bridge, Wagon Wheel, Good's Bridge, Canby Community Park riverbank and Meadowbrook Bridge. Canby Pond's age restriction does not apply to the separate riverbank record.
- Lake Oswego, Milwaukie and West Linn: Tryon Cove, Milwaukie Bay, Rivervilla, Spring Park/Elk Rock Island, Mary S. Young mainland, Cedar Island, Maddax, Burnside, West Bridge, Sportcraft, Clackamette Cove and Lower Millennium's public Oswego Lake platforms.
- Lower Tualatin: River Run, Shipley, Fields Bridge, Swiftshore and the Michael Court fishing platform.
- Wilsonville through Newberg: Molalla River State Park, Hebb, the two separate Boones Ferry accesses, Memorial Park, Wilsonville Pond, Woodburn Pond, Champoeg, Rogers and Butteville.
- Salem edge: San Salvador, Wheatland east bank, Ediger west bank, Mission Lake, Spong's, Keizer Rapids, Wallace Marine, Salem Riverfront, Spring Valley, Walter Wirth, Walling, Silverton Reservoir, Turner Lake and Minto-Brown.

## Inventory and new source classes

Compared the named candidates with the complete Clackamas County park fishing and launch listings, relevant city river-access inventories, Oregon State Parks profiles and maps, ODFW bank-fishing and warmwater inventories, and the Marine Board facility layer supplied by root. The layer includes private moorages, pumpouts, historical whitewater put-ins and entries with no public parking. Those records were evaluated by function instead of being treated as equivalent fishing destinations. Additional source classes used in this pass were the Clackamas and Tualatin water trails, Willamette Riverkeeper's landing inventory, the AccessTrails ground-level accessibility account for Fields Bridge, current municipal dock rules, PGE park maps, city capital-project reporting, dated local reporting about public Oswego Lake fishing, and the live OHA advisory layer. The live layer matters because the indexed advisory table contains stale Portland status. USGS NAIP imagery was downloaded and visually inspected for each included access. Owner and trail information supplied the legal basis. Several initial coordinates were corrected after the imagery showed a park centroid, an industrial frontage or the wrong side of a river. All supplied pins are approximate shore-access or parking-area pins. They do not claim an exact dry casting step at current water level. Four otherwise supported public fishing facilities have no precise parking or casting pin: Eagle Fern, Tualatin River Open Space at Michael Court, Turner Lake and Minto-Brown slough platforms. In each case, the owner supplies public fishing access, while canopy or development imagery prevented a defensible exact pin. Their named access directions and scores remain in the app. Null pins should not turn those whole locations into an “unverified” warning.

## Rating approach

Ratings concern the selected access. Personal space measures room for anglers to separate; road noise affects quiet setting. The water score concerns the chosen margin or cove rather than the strongest current anywhere in the river. A stocked lake can score well despite a modest research-confidence score, and a thoroughly documented tiny launch can still score poorly for personal space. Each record has dated sources, a score reason for every factor, a twelve-month seasonal profile and a short research log requiring future reassessment. Profiles are seasonal estimates rather than claimed automatic observations. Closure overrides are dated and separate from the long-term keeper assessment. The app's existing weights and definitions are unchanged. Root's routing screen informed the 90-minute Salem-edge cases. They are not live traffic promises, and an island snapping to a nearby road is not used as evidence of land access.

## Remaining named gaps

The inventory preserves deferred decisions for Roehr, Foothills, Estacada city shoreline, Glen Avon, the hatchery-side Eagle Creek bank, Snuffin Road, Pettit and Goose Lake. The problems are specific: dock prohibitions, unfinished access, public-route boundaries or lack of a practical bank position. Goose Lake has a real crappie fishery, but the public description favors carried watercraft and its undeveloped trail does not establish a useful casting bank. Technical upper-stream put-ins were excluded where accepted trout must be released and no alternative accepted keeper fishery was established. Youth-only ponds, private marina docks and boat-in islands also have explicit decisions. Existing bank records are mapped by ID, and aliases such as Dahl Beach, Bernert Landing and Tulip Road are accounted for.
