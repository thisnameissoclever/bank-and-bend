Reviewed November 1, 2026. Added grays-covered-bridge-north-bank after a coverage omission: the corpus had Satterlund and Rosburg but no covered-bridge access. September 17, 2026 reporting in The Eagle identifies the north-end landowner and continued public permission. The 2021 Focus visitor guide describes fishing access on both sides. The south-side Ahlberg Park is separate from this north-side approach. This is permissive private access, with the permission report explicitly attached to the listing.
