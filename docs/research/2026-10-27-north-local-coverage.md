# North and west local coverage, checked October 27, 2026

Each included record has a monthly profile, dated reasoning, cited sources, approximate parking and shore-entry points, and documented aerial inspection. Personal space concerns usable physical room; highway and industrial noise affect quietness. The records cover Division Street and Mill Street in Ridgefield; Vancouver Lake Regional Park and the separate south WDFW launch; Frenchman's Bar, Blurock and Langsdorf; Salmon Creek's northwest Vancouver greenway; Martin WDFW/Pekin Ferry, private Pekin Ferry, Austin Point and Martin Bar; Goerig/Hayes Bridge; Paradise Point, Two Forks, C.D. Hale, John Pollock and Timmen's Landing. Division Street is the stronger Ridgefield fit for physical room and gentle water. Mill Street remains visible as a distinct easy-access bank with limited ramp-side room. The records make no claim about an angler having visited either place. Pins were interpreted from named public-access sources and USGS NAIP exports. Their uncertainty is explicitly about 30-100 meters. The aerial capture date was not exposed. Layout can be checked visually; current vegetation, river level and property signs cannot. Vancouver Lake Regional Park, Two Forks and Salmon Creek have the most uncertainty about an exact dry casting opening. Two Forks personal space is null because tree cover prevented a defensible estimate.

## Access identities and restrictions

- Martin WDFW Boat Ramp and WDFW Pekin Ferry 1219 are one site. The private Pekin Ferry park across the Lewis is separate and requires host registration and current fee/event checks.
- Goerig Park and Hayes Road Bridge shore launch are one site.
- Timmen's Landing and John Pollock are distinct opposite-bank La Center access points. No evidence connected “Tern Place” to either name.
- C.D. Hale is on the lowermost East Fork. A regional directory's loose confluence description must not cause application of main-Lewis rules there.
- East Fork Lewis access is closed in April and August, with partial closures in March, May, July and September. Selective gear prohibits bait, although compliant conventional lures remain possible. Cutthroat and wild rainbow release restrictions limit keeper fit.
- Vancouver Lake's flushing-channel closure is at the lake end, within 400 feet north and south, April 1-May 31. Blurock is at the separate Columbia mouth.
- Martin Bar's public strip is constrained by Port signs; adjacent industrial leases are excluded. Austin Point planning proposals are not described as completed facilities. Current rules: [western freshwater WAC](https://app.leg.wa.gov/WAC/default.aspx?cite=220-312-030), [Columbia WAC](https://app.leg.wa.gov/WAC/default.aspx?cite=220-312-060), [emergency index](https://wdfw.wa.gov/fishing/regulations/emergency-rules). The live emergency index and September 24 Columbia update were read. The September additional Chinook days are not described as an October Chinook opening at these banks. The [October 26 WDFW creel report](https://wdfw.wa.gov/sites/default/files/2026-10/swwa-fishing-reports-oct-19-25-2026.pdf) documents Lewis River bank anglers retaining salmon. It does not identify these individual banks. Vancouver and Woodland mainstem sections had no sampled bank effort, so their boat catches were not used as shore catch rates. Vancouver Lake's [county alert entry](https://gis.clark.wa.gov/arcgisfed/rest/services/Hosted/Regional_Water_Alerts_RO/FeatureServer/0/query?where=1%3D1&outFields=*&returnGeometry=false&f=pjson) reported no current advisory and was last edited October 7. It did not provide a recent sample date. The lake records use none-reported with that limitation.
