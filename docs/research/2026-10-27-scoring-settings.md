# Shared scoring settings

The revised starting baseline assigns quiet setting 3, casting quality 3 and ease of access 7. Other retained factors keep their previous values. Travel fit is removed from calculations, score filters and score sorting. Drive time remains visible and filterable, with its own sort option. Legacy travel thresholds are discarded and legacy travel-score sorting becomes drive-time sorting. The owner can change any factor using an independent 0-100 importance slider. The shared `scoring_settings` table stores one site-wide configuration with revision-checked writes. Public readers receive the same settings; only the configured owner's authenticated identity can edit them. The browser recalculates immediately, serializes rapid saves, reports failed writes and rejects conflicting stale writes. Other open pages refresh on focus and every 30 seconds. The public corpus and research tools use the same configuration. Journals and filter preferences retain their own storage. Each factor has a definition beside its slider. The same definitions appear in score details and the research panel, with rating anchors available in the research panel, corpus and `docs/SCORING.md`. Physical personal space concerns usable room and separation. Audible traffic, boats and recreation belong to quiet setting. Manageable water concerns controlling a useful bait, float, spinner or lure presentation at the supported bank. Ordinary gentle water can score highly even beside a fast main channel. Tests cover storage persistence across independent store instances, owner authorization, competing writes, independent importance values, invalid settings, missing-factor redistribution, ranking changes, score filtering and legacy preference migration. Desktop and 390-pixel mobile component previews check slider behavior and description layout. The preview does not simulate the hosted sign-in flow; authenticated authorization is tested at the storage boundary and anonymous hosted access is checked after deployment.
