# Oregon east coverage audit, October 27, 2026

Added 26 shore-fishing candidates across east metro, the Sandy watershed, the Columbia Gorge and the Mt. Hood approach. The companion inventory contains 125 decisions, including launch aliases, existing records, closed candidates, access holds and outer-range exclusions. The strongest additions for easy water include Mt. Hood Community College Pond, the paid Rainbow Trout Farm, sheltered Herman Creek Lagoon and the Hook, plus Kingsley Reservoir near the two-hour edge. Barlow provides a quieter Sandy gravel bank. Glenn Otto is useful outside swimming crowds. Scores reflect judgments about the selected position, separately from research confidence.

## Sources and method

Compared ODFW's Portland bank-fishing list and high-lake inventories with Oregon State Marine Board's complete public-access GIS, city/county park inventories, USFS recreation records, Port waterfront pages and regional fishing guides. Included each relevant named launch even when it was excluded as a fishing destination. Used official 2026 zone rules and the emergency-update index, OHA's live algae map data and consumption advisories. Read local angler and trail reports to resolve physical access and practical fishing limitations. Viewed USGS NAIP aerial images for every added shore, including parking approaches. The image URLs and specific support are saved in each record. Imagery dates were not established. The shore pins are approximate supported positions; they do not certify today's waterline or footing. River speed describes the selected margin or eddy. Main-channel current is described separately.

## Material findings

- **Cedar Creek/Sandy bank:** Sandy River Park closed after a bear encounter on September 24. Searches of city pages, police-related reporting and local news did not locate a dated reopening. The city and hatchery trails converge near the same fishing bank, so those aliases are consolidated and the candidate remains inactive. The exact scope of hatchery-only access is the remaining issue.
- **Dalton Point:** The ramp is closed for damage. The park plan separately identifies shore fishing. The bank remains a candidate, with the specific possibility of expanded work fencing disclosed.
- **Clear Lake, Mt. Hood:** Campground and day use closed September 30. Retained inactive for its open season. A summer fishing report describes severe irrigation drawdown.
- **Frog Lake:** The seasonal vehicle area is closed. The entry instead uses the year-round Sno-Park and its established lake route. This adds a walk. Summer green-water observations are recorded as observations, without converting them into a toxin advisory.
- **Kingsley:** County confirmation of 3,500 legal trout delivered April 14, 2026 is stronger evidence than an undated fish-presence claim. October 19 camping closure does not by itself close the day-use bank.
- **Marmot:** BLM public access and the fishable gravel bank are supported. Conflicting map locations and canopy prevented a reliable exact parking coordinate despite manager, paddling and aerial checks. The parking pin is null; the destination remains visible.
- **Revenue Bridge:** An external geographic-name index placed Revenue Bridge on the Bull Run. That index was rejected after ODFW identified Revenue as the Ten Eyck Sandy crossing and the Kubitz/Ten Eyck intersection was independently located. The original Marine Board shoreline was correct. Final parking is approximately 45.40757, -122.23753.
- **Barlow:** The county embedded map centers away from the actual lot. Trailhead coordinates, aerial clearing and the signed 260-yard river spur resolve the parking route. The bank lies below the Salmon confluence.
- **Herman Creek:** The lagoon is distinct from the eastern creek channel and the youth/disabled hatchery channel. Their seasonal closures and boundaries are stated explicitly.
- **Middle Columbia:** Resident fish from Ruckel Creek through McNary have a one-meal-per-week advisory, with no other fish that week. Salmon and steelhead are excluded. Applicable Gorge entries display this as an active fish-consumption advisory.
- **Mt. Hood Pond:** All-ages access is September through March. April through August is reserved for youth or qualifying disabled anglers. Fall stocking amounts are schedules, without an invented delivery confirmation or remaining-fish count.

## Coverage holds and exclusions

The inventory explains every named decision. Meaningful unresolved access holds include the private Fishery marina, gated Corbett Station, the former Bull Run power-site access, Government Cove's tribal peninsula, the new raised walkway at Sharon Nesbit Heritage Park, several railroad-separated creek mouths and the roadside Hood River RM 6 put-in. These are legal-access or practical-bank questions, not a demand for perfect catch documentation. Upper Sandy, Zigzag, accessible lower Salmon and upper Hood tributary put-ins generally fail the keeper-fish requirement under the applicable native-trout rules. Bradford Island has public angling but steep tailrace banks and exceptionally forceful water. Existing shore records absorb launch duplicates. Rainy, Wahtum, Lost, Laurance, Boulder and Badger lakes exceed the current travel envelope in the route screen.
