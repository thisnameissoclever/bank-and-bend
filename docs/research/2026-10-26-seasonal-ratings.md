# Seasonal ratings and bank preferences

The overall rating helps choose an upcoming bank-fishing trip. General keeper potential and current-season prospects each receive 20%. Usable bank receives 16%, personal space 12%, manageable water 10%, quiet setting 7%, scenery 7%, travel 3%, ease of access 2%, species 2% and casting quality 1%. Research confidence remains separate. Casting quality has no independent score cap. Each location has a normal-year monthly calendar supported by source references and a stated judgment. Recent reports can override that calendar for a dated interval. The current Pacific date selects the rating automatically, including when an open tab crosses midnight. Expired reports return to the calendar. A null score displays as a question mark, contributes no weight and rescales the remaining factors. The details show the effective percentage for each rated factor. Existing saved defaults migrate to these weights. Custom weights, filters, sorting and journals remain intact. The saved-weight revision prevents later intentional choices from being mistaken for old defaults. High Rocks receives 8 for the selected pool edge's manageable water.
