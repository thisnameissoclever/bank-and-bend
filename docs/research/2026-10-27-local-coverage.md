# Local fishing access coverage

The October 27 review expands the local inventory around northwest Vancouver through municipal park lists, agency fishing inventories, public launch directories and inspected aerial imagery. It includes suitable and lower-fit candidates so search and filters can support an angler's decisions. Separate access points retain separate records where their parking, usable bank or practical fit differs. Regional evidence and considered-site inventories are recorded in:

- `2026-10-27-north-local-coverage.md`: Ridgefield, Lake River, lower Lewis River, west Vancouver and the nearby northern corridor.
- `2026-10-27-east-local-coverage.md`: east Vancouver, Camas, Washougal and Washington Gorge access.
- `2026-10-27-oregon-local-coverage.md`: Portland, the Willamette, Sauvie Island, Columbia County and Oregon Gorge banks. Each included location stores source URLs, retrieval dates, parking and shore-entry notes, visible-layout evidence, factor scores and their reasoning, a monthly seasonal profile and material restrictions. Aerial inspections establish physical layout. Approximate pins remain labeled approximate. Old photographs and historical catches retain their dates. Closure and retention rules apply independently of an overall fit score. Steamboat Landing is included at an angler's explicit request. Its public dock is described clearly; the general Columbia-pier exclusion remains in place for additional candidates. The Division Street launch and Mill Street ramp in Ridgefield are separate accesses. Fish-consumption limits on the lower Willamette affect the accepted dinner targets and are displayed in location details. Icehouse Lake's personal-space rating changes from 3 to 5. Its east and south access corridor provides room for several possible openings, with vegetation and reported heavy pressure limiting flexibility. The quiet-setting score retains the traffic-noise assessment. The score definitions are in `docs/SCORING.md` and appear beside every settings slider.

## Continuing the inventory

At each expansion, compare the corpus against authoritative local park, launch and fishing inventories in all travel directions. Revisit named gaps in the regional reports. Distinguish a completed review that found a concrete restriction from a candidate whose review remains incomplete. Do available follow-up research before carrying a material uncertainty forward. Keep each unresolved issue specific and preserve its source trail. Do not use a low score or a missing exact-position catch report as grounds to omit a legitimate candidate. Assess accepted keeper species, current season, legal access, practical casting positions, physical room, manageable water and travel separately. A good park setting alone does not establish a useful fishery. Previous rating explanations remain hypotheses to reassess against current evidence.
