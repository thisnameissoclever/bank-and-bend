# Oregon east and Gorge access audit

Checked 2026-10-28. All 37 records received manager/access-source review and aerial inspection at every supplied original parking and fishing coordinate. New points were chosen from visible land, then georeferenced to the actual square export extent. Imagery dates were not supplied. Ownership/access conclusions rely on public-manager descriptions, maps and relevant access guides. The retained access judgments remain open to correction. Historical satellite views do not establish current water levels, gates or trail damage. Closure and hours findings are stated per location.
