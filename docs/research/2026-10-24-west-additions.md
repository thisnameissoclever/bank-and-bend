Its North Shore reopening line carries an older, undated December 23 annotation, so the record describes the current listing without inventing a reopening date. A new source type, the City of Tacoma engineering drawing, establishes the parking, path, stairs, concrete walk and railings. Page 56 was rendered and inspected. Satellite imagery and a ground photograph were inspected as well. Parking pin: 46.536888, -122.424928. Fishing pin: 46.536557, -122.424783. The fishing pin locates the public walkway; October waterline and landing reach remain uncertain. The older ground photo shows higher water. The October 6 Sportsman's Warehouse report explicitly supports dam-bank bait fishing. Its trolling depths describe a different method, and its ramp statement is superseded by the operator. ## Swofford Pond northeast bank

WDFW confirms public wildlife-area and water access plus good shoreline fishing. The WDFW stocking row records 5,400 rainbows on May 20, 2026 at 2.70 fish per pound, averaging about six ounces. Remaining fish counts and present fish sizes are unknown. An angler report from May 3 involved a boat. Its method is preserved in the record. Satellite inspection showed gravel launch parking and a bank opening immediately west. Parking: 46.502449, -122.391919. Approximate bank opening: 46.502523, -122.392219. A detailed local guide identifies northeast/east bank access and weeds. The route is short; current weed height and exact footing remain uncertain. Drive estimate: 95 to 115 minutes, with no live route-time claim. Sources:

## Deep Lake, Millersylvania State Park

WDFW describes good bank access and stocked-rainbow opportunity. The April 22, 2026 plant was 2,500 rainbows at 2.15 fish per pound, about 7.4 ounces each. Official park hours are 8 a.m. to dusk in winter. Discover Pass is required for state-park day use. The official brochure map and current satellite view were inspected. Parking: 46.909345, -122.915782. Approximate northwestern bank opening: 46.909089, -122.915106. The point lies east of the dock and outside the visible western swimming enclosure. Canopy obscures final footing. The park's 3,300 feet of shoreline includes vegetation and swimming areas, so the record does not equate it with castable bank. Drive estimate: 95 to 115 minutes. The current Thurston County advisory table, checked October 24, lists Lake Lawrence, Offutt, Pattison and Clear. Deep Lake is absent. The old September 2021 Deep Lake warning was excluded from current status. A recent sample or clearance date remains unknown. Sources:

- https://wdfw.wa.gov/fishing/locations/lowland-lakes/deep-lake-thurston
- https://parks.wa.gov/find-parks/state-parks/millersylvania-state-park
- https://parks.wa.gov/sites/default/files/2023-05/Millersylvania%20State%20Park%20Brochure%2012-27-21.pdf
- https://wdfw.wa.gov/fishing/reports/stocking/trout-plants/all?page=5&region=6
- https://www.thurstoncountywa.gov/departments/public-health-and-social-services/environmental-health/water/water-recreation

Assessment: strong keeper potential and manageable water. Forest and public shore length support reasonable personal-space and quietness scores. These are judgments, especially the expectation of fewer October swimmers.

## Shared legal and health checks

The 2026 statewide species and freshwater rules support year-round unlisted lake fishing, five trout daily with no minimum, and counting all legal trout caught while using bait. Riffe's specific entry confirms year-round fishing at the selected dam-side access and landlocked-salmon rules. Smallmouth daily limit is 10, with only one over 14 inches. Perch and crappie follow statewide no-minimum, no-limit rules here.
