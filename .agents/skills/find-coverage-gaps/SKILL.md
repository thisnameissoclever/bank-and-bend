---
name: find-coverage-gaps
description: Find omitted Bank & Bend access points inside the existing Washington and Oregon coverage envelope without enlarging it.
---

Read docs/COVERAGE.md, docs/ACCESS.md and the latest dated coverage inventory. Reuse the documented envelope, public screening origin and road-time method. Prioritize interior gaps within 90 minutes, then the existing outer range. Do not add another buffer merely because the last expansion used one. A drive filter is not a coverage polygon.

Compare existing IDs, coordinates, aliases and unresolved/excluded leads with WDFW water-access sites, ODFW fishing inventories, Oregon Marine Board launches, parks, ports, utilities, land trusts, easements and paid operators. Use the source URLs in data/coverage-audit.json and prior expansion files. Search by corridor and county in all directions, including east and west along the Columbia. Check launches for a legal adjacent bank; distinguish a pier fishing the mainstem from access to a tributary. Run a second independent inventory across gaps.

For each lead, record included, duplicate, excluded or unresolved with a reason and evidence. Separate banks only if they offer materially different access. Verify keeper species, manager, legal parking, walk, dry casting position, rules, fees and imagery. Do not guess pins. Keep unresolved access inactive and explain it. Use the existing spot schema and stable IDs, assess every applicable score, and update coverage/access audits and research notes. Follow docs/UPDATE-PROTOCOL.md through tests and live readback.
