---
name: audit-spots
description: Audit all details of existing Bank & Bend locations, including pins, routes, scores, rules, advisories and effective availability.
---

Read AGENTS.md and docs/{MIGRATION-STATUS,UPDATE-PROTOCOL,ACCESS,IMAGERY,SCORING}.md. Start from current main, production /api/corpus and /api/availability. Build a checklist keyed by every stable ID, including inactive and paid properties. Resume from a checkpoint, starting with outstanding IDs. A successful URL fetch alone does not complete a review.

1. Check the manager, hours, fees, season, gate, visitors allowed, parking and continuous legal walk. Inspect aerials, parcel/access maps and ground photos. Verify parking and dry casting positions separately, walking time, steep banks, vegetation, logs, private boundaries and launch interference. Open all three Maps destinations; the named place must resolve to the right access. Record imagery date if known and explain missing pins.
2. Verify species, methods, retention, emergency changes, water type, current at the bank, seasonal water levels and consumption or bloom advisories. Use docs/research/2026-10-27-advisories.md for monitoring queries. Separate sample, posting and retrieval dates. Follow up missing data through another useful source. A swim advisory can have different consequences from a fishing closure.
3. Reconsider every current score using lib/score-definitions.ts. Judge visibility from paths and opposite banks, usable shoreline, casting clearance, access effort, water control, scenery, seasonal keeper prospects and confidence. Inspect both physical and privacy limits. Preserve firsthand observations, nulls when warranted and current user weights. Use the stocking and prospects skills for those fields. Verify routing estimates to parking, never publish a home location.
4. When an obstacle blocks access, explain its practical effect. Reopen only on dated evidence resolving the obstacle. Preserve future closures; clear obsolete inactiveFrom on reopening. Use revision-checked owner writes on production, reconcile conflicts, reread each accepted write. If unauthorized, save the expected revision, proposed status, checkedAt, nextReviewAt, reason and sourceUrls. Never substitute a corpus edit or legacy connector.
5. Check duplicate IDs, aliases, shared-water consistency, source links and review dates. Preserve journals, filters, reviews, sharing and schedules. Account for each ID as verified, corrected or incomplete with its exact blocker. Publish tested public research per protocol; verify changed public records.
